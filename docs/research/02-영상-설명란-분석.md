### 5. Parser design

File: `tools/parser/parse_desc.py` (stdlib only; loads build.py read-only).

- **Pass 1, marker lines** `^\s*[◑▶●■◆◎☞]\s*KEY(\(basis\))?\s*[:：]?\s*VALUE`:
  - Keys are matched longest-first against an alias table, e.g. 매물번호/소재지/위치 → addr, 매매가/금액/가격 → price, 토지면적/토지 합계/매매면적/면적 → land, 건축면적/연면적/건평/주택 → building, 방/욕실, 방향, 주차, 용도지역/용도구역/토지이용계획, 진입도로, 기반시설/수도/식수/물, 준공/사용승인, 분할 매매.
  - A prefix guard keeps 방향 from being read as 방.
  - Marker lines with no key, and indented continuation lines (e.g. "방2.화장실2", "150평 매매가 1억2천만원", "지하1층 지상2층"), are handled separately.
  - Short summary lines like "매물번호 X / 주소 / … / 매매 Y" are also parsed.
- **Pass 2, free-text fallback:** fills only fields still empty, from the title (hashtags stripped) and then the description body. Office lines, hashtag lines and "•" chip lines are removed first.
- **Every field records its source:** marker, marker-extra, slash-line, title, text, hashtag, computed or inferred.
- **Money (in 만원):** an `AMT_RE` that handles 억, 억+천, 억+N,000만, N천만 and N만. "N천 원" (e.g. the 5천원 electricity bill) is excluded.
  - Price history: "A----B으로" or "A → B" → current B, previous A; "(X에서 인하)" → previous X.
  - "~" → a range.
  - Other parenthetical amounts are kept as alternative offers, with nested parentheses handled.
  - 평당 is stored as a separate value.
  - "N억대" is flagged as approximate.
  - A total is computed from 평당 × 평 only when no total exists.
- **Area:** a ㎡ value is paired with a following (평) value. 평-only values are converted at 0.3025 and flagged as computed. A ㎡/평 mismatch of more than 2% is flagged. Road share is recorded as included ("포함") or added ("+").
- **Address:** split into 시도/시군구/읍면 (validated against the 12 양평 읍면) and 리/마을. 지번 and "(상세주소…)" are stripped, and a 지번 found in the source raises `jibun_in_source`. 시도/시군구 inferred from the 읍면 are flagged.
- **Type:** building fields take priority, then title before body, with a land-first word order when there are no building fields. "임야에 접한", "국유림 접" and "자연림" are neutralised so they are not read as 임야.
- **Direction:** normalised to the site's vocabulary (동남향 → 남동향); the basis is kept from the key's parentheses.
- **Output:** `extracted.json` holds per-video fields, sources, flags, an `as_listing_draft` record in the listings.json schema, the `disclosure` result, the groups with enrichment and conflicts, and the coverage table.
- **Grouping** uses a union of: chip-title match, same raw 매물번호 within the same 읍면, same 읍면 + area within 1% + same price, an alternative price matching another video's main price (split sale), and same 리 + area with a different price (re-listed).

### 6. Known failure modes

1. **The collector loses data before parsing.** `update_videos.py` cuts descriptions at `DESC_MAX=2000` characters; 4 info videos hit the limit, and a long listing description would lose its office block. The innertube `attributedDescription.content` field also truncates links to "https://blog.naver.com/gayayangpyeong...", so the per-post blog URL is lost in every case (0 intact). The real URLs are probably in the field's commandRuns; that needs checking against a real response.
2. **매물번호 schemes are mixed and coded:** 금768, 옥150, 해195 and 다문 164 encode village plus 평; 350 matches the price; 455, 200 and 1200 match the 평. The numbers can collide, and the same property's number changed (1450 in 2022, none in 2026).
   - build.py's `NO_RE` only matches 3–6 digits right after "매물번호 :", so 금768 never links to a listing.
   - **This is a real risk: `closedListingNos` cannot hide a 금768 video once it is sold.** Only `hiddenVideos` or `notListingVideos` would work.
   - Sold detection with `DONE_RE` only looks at the title and the first 300 characters of the description.
3. **지목 is almost never stated.** "농지", "전원주택지" and the ◑대지 part-label are not legal 지목 categories.
4. **Price:** only 평당 given (qhyD), ranges (tQHU), a typo (BNUu), "3억대" in titles, and "6천인하" in a title nearly being read as a price (now handled).
5. **Area:** 평-only values give rounded ㎡ (562평 → 1,857.85 vs 1,855); "약 1,858"; road share sometimes included and sometimes added; 건축면적 used to mean 연면적; "38평+황토방8평" is ambiguous.
6. **Free text is unreliable in old videos.** Copied leftovers like "국유지로 둘러쌓인 양평 지평면…" appear in a 용문면 다문리 video and in a channel-intro Short; this is flagged as `summary_conflict`. Office lines were once misread as the property's location (fixed).
7. **Floors:** inferred from mentions (7PRVk), and conflicting in PjOh (대장 says 지하1층/지상2층, the description says "2,3층").
8. **Zoning:** "제1종 주거지역" does not say 일반 or 전용. 농업진흥지역, 농업보호구역 and 자연취락지구 are not 용도지역 categories.
9. **Unfilled placeholders were published:** "[본편 주소 — 공개 뒤 자동 입력]" in 2 Shorts and "[타임스탬프 — 영상 완성 뒤 자동 입력]" in 1 long-form.

### 7. Recommended description template

This makes extraction deterministic. Paste it as the first block of the description so it stays within 2,000 characters and the first 300.

```

◑ 거래상태 : 거래중              ← change to 계약완료 when sold → disappears from the site automatically
◑ 소재지 : 경기도 양평군 용문면 금곡리   ← always down to 리; never the 지번
◑ 매물종류 : 전원주택            ← one of 토지/임야/전원주택/농가주택/단독주택/상가/공장·창고
◑ 거래형태 : 매매                ← 매매/전세/월세
◑ 가격 : 4억 9,500만원           ← a single amount only (월세: 보증금 1,000만원 / 월 50만원); 평당 price goes on its own ◑ 평당가 line
```

**Rules that go with the template:**
- Use exactly these keys, one fact per line.
